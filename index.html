Mind Fuel 100% Focus ✦ ★ ✧

The assignment

The Identity Design Challenge is an individual design project in which I developed a complete visual identity for a self-created beverage brand. The challenge was to translate an abstract core value into a clear and consistent visual language, showing how a brand strategy can be translated into a clear and consistent visual identity.

My approach

For my concept, I chose mathematics as the core value and used this as the starting point for the entire design process. From this core value, I developed the energetic and recognisable identity of Mind Fuel. I combined mathematical elements, bold numbers and a strong blue colour palette with playful orange details to create a distinctive visual language. The can design brings these elements together into a clear and memorable brand identity.

Design & development

I explored different ways to translate the concept into visual elements by experimenting with typography, shapes and graphic compositions. I focused on creating a strong visual balance between the structured nature of mathematics and a playful, energetic appearance. I then applied the developed visual style across the different brand components to create a consistent and recognisable design.

The assignment

The Game Dev Challenge is an individual game development project in which I created a 2D side-scroller game in Unity. The challenge was to design a playable game with a unique visual style, interactive gameplay, obstacles and a scoring system, inspired by the mechanics of Flappy Bird.

My approach

For this project, I wanted to combine the familiar gameplay of Flappy Bird with the colourful and playful atmosphere of Angry Birds. My main focus was to create a game that feels familiar but still has its own character. I explored how the characters, backgrounds and obstacles could work together to create a recognisable visual identity. I also aimed to keep the gameplay simple and accessible, while adding extra elements such as collectible items to make the experience more interesting.

Design & development

The game was built in Unity using C#. During development, I translated the concept into a playable experience by programming the core systems, including character movement, obstacle spawning, collision handling and score tracking. I tested and refined different aspects of the gameplay to improve responsiveness and balance the difficulty. I also integrated the visual assets into the game environment, making sure that the design and technical elements worked well together.

NS App Redesign ✦ ★ ✧

The assignment

The Gebruikscontext – Ontwerp challenge focuses on adapting an interaction design to different user contexts. The goal is to understand how factors such as the environment, user needs and technology influence the way people interact with a digital product.

My approach

For this challenge, I chose to redesign the NS app, focusing on how different usage contexts influence the way people interact with digital products. The goal was to explore how an interface can respond to the needs of travellers in different situations. I compared two usage contexts, looking at how factors such as environment, time pressure and user needs affect the overall experience.

Design & development

Based on the two usage contexts, I developed design solutions to improve the NS app. I focused on adapting the interface to the specific needs of travellers in each situation. This included reconsidering the presentation of information, the navigation and the placement of important functions. I aimed to create an interface that is clear, intuitive and easy to use, regardless of the context.

5.✦

The assignment

The The Wat vind jij? project is an interaction design concept for the Roald museum app, developed to make museum visits more engaging for young adults aged 18–25. The goal was to encourage visitors to actively think about artworks and share their opinions instead of only listening to information.

My approach

Together with my teammate, I developed an interactive concept for the Roald museum app, featuring three different ways to respond to artworks. Visitors can tap on an object, answer a yes-or-no question or express their opinion on a scale. They can then compare their answers with those of other visitors and discover the story behind the artwork. The concept was developed into an interactive prototype within a copy of the existing Roald app, allowing visitors to experience the interactions on a phone.

Research & user experience

My main focus was on designing the overall user experience for the target audience (18–25). I worked on mapping out the user journey, exploring how visitors move through the app and experience the different interactions. I considered the needs, expectations and behaviour of the target audience to create a logical flow throughout the experience. My goal was to make the museum visit more engaging by encouraging visitors to actively participate and interact with the artworks.

The assignment

The Pro Website Challenge is an individual web design project in which I recreated an existing website using HTML and CSS. The challenge was to analyse an existing website and translate its visual design into a functional and visually accurate webpage, paying close attention to layout, positioning, typography, colours and overall style.

My approach

For this project, I chose an existing website as the starting point for my design process. Before starting the development, I analysed the website's structure, visual hierarchy and overall appearance. I also made a sketch of the layout to understand how the different elements were positioned and how I could recreate the page. I paid particular attention to the use of colours, typography, spacing and proportions to create a clear plan for the development process.

Design & development

I translated my initial analysis and sketches into a webpage using HTML and CSS. I used CSS Grid and Flexbox to build the layout and position the different elements. During development, I experimented with fonts, spacing, sizes and styling to get as close as possible to the original design. I also tested the webpage in the browser and made adjustments to improve the visual accuracy of the final result.
